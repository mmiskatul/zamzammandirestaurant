# Zam Zam Dubai

ZAM ZAM MANDI RESTAURANT

Premium Multi-Page Restaurant Website — Nadd Al Hamar, Dubai

Create a premium, modern, aesthetically sophisticated restaurant website for:

ZAM ZAM MANDI RESTAURANT (NADD AL HAMAR)

Location: Rebat St, near Bel Remaitha Club, Nadd Al Hamar, Dubai, United Arab Emirates
Phone: +971 4 892 9060
Cuisine: Authentic Yemeni / Arabian / Middle Eastern
Price positioning: AED 50–100 per person
Opening hours: 11:00 AM – 12:00 AM daily

The restaurant is known for authentic Mandi and Yemeni cuisine, generous portions, family-friendly dining, traditional seating, and dishes such as Chicken Mandi, Mutton Haneeth, Chicken Madbee, ribs, Luqaimat and Arabic beverages.

The website should feel like a real premium Dubai restaurant website, not a generic template.
